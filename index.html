Who We Are

Founded by passionate WUT students, our goal is to break down cultural barriers. By bringing international students and Polish locals together, we create a vibrant, inclusive campus life.

What We Do

  • Cultural Immersion: Traditional food tastings and holiday celebrations.
  • Social Integration: Board game nights, pub crawls, and language exchanges.
  • Student Support: Guides to surviving exams and finding the best student discounts.
